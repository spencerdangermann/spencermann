The Nail — Hollow Knight Sword Cosplay (TPU)

Free STL by Spencer Mann (Spencermann) — original design on MakerWorld.

The Nail is Hollow Knight's iconic sword, modeled to sit next to the Knight and Hornet masks. The files are set up for TPU so the blade has a little flex for cosplay instead of a brittle PLA stick.

The Nail from Hollow Knight. I designed this sword to compliment the masks and other weapons I have designed for Hollow Knight. I always try to base the creations as closely as I can on the video game art, in this case the sword is really not that big of a weapon. Tags: hollow knight, sword, video game, COSPLAY.
