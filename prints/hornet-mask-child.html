Hornet Mask — Child & Teen Hollow Knight Cosplay

Free STL by Spencer Mann (Spencermann) — original design on MakerWorld.

The child and teen Hornet Mask is the original size I designed from Hollow Knight screenshots so the proportions stay true to the 2D art. If you are printing for a kid, a smaller adult, or a display head, start here instead of the large Silksong adult mask.

This is the Hornet mask from Hollow Knight. I used actual video game images (2d) to determine the dimensions to try and get it as accurate as possible. Tags: hollow knight, hornet, mask, cosplay.
