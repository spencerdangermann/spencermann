Hollow Knight Mask — Adult Cosplay 3D Print

Free STL by Spencer Mann (Spencermann) — original design on MakerWorld.

This is the adult Hollow Knight Mask — the Knight's own helmet, enlarged from the youth version after people asked for a head that actually fits grown-ups. If you want the classic bug-head look rather than Hornet or Grimm, this is the print.

I had previously created a “youth size” of this mask , but due to community support, I decided to update it and make it sized for adults. I also made multiple improvements to the quality of the print (*learning*). Notable improvements over the last version: It's bigger.
