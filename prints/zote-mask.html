Zote the Mighty Mask — Hollow Knight 3D Print

Free STL by Spencer Mann (Spencermann) — original design on MakerWorld.

Zote the Mighty is the newest Hollow Knight mask in the collection. The shell is built a little differently from Hornet and the Knight so the pretender's face reads correctly in person. Print it as a gag cosplay or to complete a Hallownest mask wall.

This one is for Squidward who relentlessly pushed for me to make the model, and then was so grateful when I got started! I hope you enjoy it! I designed this mask a bit different from my others and I am happy with the outcome. Tags: Hollow Knight, hollow knight, zote, mask.
