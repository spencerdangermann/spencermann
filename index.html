About Me

Welcome! I'm Spencer Mann (Spencermann). I'm an engineer by training, and I design and test-print these models at home — often with my kids — on Bambu Lab printers. A lot of my work is Hollow Knight and Silksong cosplay (masks, needles, nails), plus Glitch Productions figures, working water fountains, and everyday utility prints. Every file I publish is free on MakerWorld.

Why people download Spencermann prints

  • Original designs — modeled from in-game art, prototype-printed, then posted as the free MakerWorld release.
  • 100+ free models as @spencermann on MakerWorld, including 36 Hollow Knight / Silksong prints.
  • Popular Creator on MakerWorld (1,000+ followers). The adult Hornet Mask is the most-liked design on this site.
  • Built to print — Bambu Lab profiles, split parts, and sizes for kids and adults on the flagship masks.
