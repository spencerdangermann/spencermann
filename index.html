About Me

Welcome! I'm Spencer Mann, and I'm passionate about creating and sharing my work with others. A lot of my work is focused on 3d design and printing. My journey into 3D design started with a love for bringing digital creations into the physical world, and I've found particular joy in crafting detailed cosplay pieces, especially from beloved games like Hollow Knight. Every design is crafted with attention to detail and tested to ensure quality prints. I'm constantly working on new projects and love seeing what the community creates with my designs. Feel free to reach out and share your creations!
